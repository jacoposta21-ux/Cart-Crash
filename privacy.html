Privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Cart Crash is a free browser game. You play without an account and the game has no server of its own.

What is stored

Your best score, your furthest distance, the number of rides, your language and your sound settings are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What is not collected

The game itself collects no name, email address, location or device identifier, uses no cookies of its own, no analytics and no advertising of its own.

When you play on a game portal

If you play Cart Crash on a game portal such as CrazyGames or Playgama, that portal runs its own services around the game (for example its account system, advertising and statistics) under its own privacy policy. The game only tells the portal when a ride starts and stops, asks it for your language and follows its mute and pause settings. Where the portal offers it, your best score and number of rides are also kept in the portal's own storage, and the portal may show an ad between rides.

Contact

For any question about privacy, write to help@trustyourbot.com.
